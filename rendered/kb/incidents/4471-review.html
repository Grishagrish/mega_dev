Engineering tickets

  • Shared parser interprets day-first dates month-first. For day-first tenants, app/email and booking-v2 form reports use the shared parser path. For example, R-41200 reports 7 April becoming 4 July. Fix the shared parser to respect the input contract and tenant locale; reject ambiguous or invalid input rather than silently changing it.
  • booking-v3 advertises month-first input to day-first tenants. For form reports from day-first tenants running booking-v3, the printed MM/DD/YYYY hint conflicts with the tenant-aware form reader. R-41230 from copper.studio is an example. Make hint, validation and parsing use the same tenant-aware format. A shared-parser fix does not repair this separate UI contract.

Verify both fixes independently with ambiguous dates such as 06/01/2026 and invalid dates. Include day-first and month-first tenants, booking-v2 and booking-v3, and app/email/form paths. Confirm that visible guidance and the resulting invite agree.

Conflicting one-day descriptions

That classification does not prove that a parser caused each narrative. Preserve these reports and request original input, stored timestamp, timezone and generated invite before asserting another root cause. Do not subtract them from the exported classifier’s counts or double-count them in a speculative third ticket.

What the desk should change

The triage page was last reviewed in June 2024 and must be revalidated against its executable examples. Proposed replacement: use subject matching only to find candidates, then compare tenant locale, booking form, channel and causal evidence. Unknown tenants remain unplaced until investigated.
